# ReachMe — The Concept

> The source of truth for what ReachMe is. Read this before
> any product or design decision. If a change contradicts any
> statement in this document, either update the change or
> update this document — but don't ship a contradiction.

---

## Who It's For

ReachMe is for anyone whose attention is already in demand —
creators, artists, founders, executives, investors, advisors,
public figures. People who don't have a problem being known.
They have a problem being reached *well*.

If you're still building an audience, this isn't for you.
ReachMe is for people who already have one and are drowning
in the noise that comes with it.

---

## The Core Idea

Your attention is your most valuable asset. The open internet
treats it as infinitely available. ReachMe doesn't.

It is not a contact form, a link-in-bio, or a social network.
It is a **serious request layer** — a structured perimeter
around valuable attention. You set your terms. Senders respect
them before they write a word.

---

## The Four Pillars

### 1. Asymmetry of Effort

Sending a cold DM costs nothing. ReachMe introduces friction —
the sender must declare their intent and attach a financial
signal that meets your floor. If they aren't serious, the
friction stops them. Noise self-selects out.

### 2. Zero Discovery, Pure Referral

No explore page. No search. No directory. Your page only exists
if you share the link. Every visitor arrives with context. This
is not a platform for building an audience — it's a tool for
managing the one you already have.

### 3. Escrow of Respect

The money isn't a fee — it's a commitment held in escrow. You
reply, they're honored. You decline or ignore within the window,
they're refunded. The platform's incentive is quality, not
volume.

### 4. Minimal by Design

Your page shows one thing: who you are, what it costs to reach
you, and one action. No bio. No tag cloud. No noise. The
categories live in the request flow — where they belong, when
intent is already present. What the sender sees first is calm,
authoritative, and complete.
